# Clean Editorial Collage

Use this preset when the user wants a crisp, restrained editorial collage, a clean retro poster, or a visual-explanation frame whose handmade character should come from cutting and layering rather than dirt and surface noise.

## Composition Budget

- Build each frame around one unmistakable primary subject.
- Add two to four supporting elements that directly advance the same visual claim.
- Reserve roughly 25–40% of the canvas as intentional negative space.
- Use three to six guide marks at most across the full frame: arrows, circles, grids, numbers, underlines, short rules, or small geometric blocks.
- Give the primary subject the largest silhouette and strongest contrast. Group supporting elements by meaning rather than scattering them evenly.
- Prefer an asymmetric editorial layout with a clear reading path. Slight offsets and overlaps are welcome; specimen boards, equal distribution, and nine-grid layouts are not.

These numbers describe visible semantic elements in the final composition. They do not forbid splitting an element into the independent layers required for later animation.

## Palette Budget

- Use black, charcoal, gray, and off-white as the neutral system.
- Add one high-chroma accent color. A second accent is optional and should occupy a visibly smaller area.
- Keep the background as one stable, even color field or one quiet paper tone.
- Restrict accents to major geometric fields, labels, arrows, underlines, or small focal details.
- Avoid gradients, rainbow palettes, decorative color variation, and broad accidental tinting of black-and-white photographs.
- Allow slight screen-print registration offset only on a few decorative color edges, never on faces, text, or critical silhouettes.

## Material and Edge Language

- Make collage depth through controlled cutting, paper rims, overlap, scale, and restrained contact shadows.
- Keep broad paper faces flat, matte, and nearly texture-free. Permit only faint, low-frequency tonal variation.
- Use clean irregular cut contours. A narrow off-white paper rim may separate a photographic subject from the background.
- Limit torn fibers to a few outer paper edges. Never let fibers, stains, or cracks spread across faces, text, color blocks, or negative space.
- Keep layers visually flat. Use very soft, close, directionally consistent contact shadows; no curled paper, floating scraps, thick extrusion, or dramatic cast shadows.
- Keep print treatment stable and legible. Halftone or photocopy grain belongs mainly inside photographic cutouts and should remain subtle enough to preserve anatomy and facial features.

## Information Graphics

Every mark must have a job:

- arrows connect cause, sequence, or comparison;
- grids organize a local relationship;
- circles and underlines emphasize one target;
- numbers establish order;
- geometric blocks anchor a group or carry the accent color.

Remove a decorative mark if the composition remains equally clear without it. Empty space is an active part of this preset and must not be filled automatically.

## Text

Follow the project's `text_mode`. For editing workflows, prefer blank paper labels plus `text-overlay.json`. If rendered text is explicitly requested, restrict the composition to the supplied headline and optional short secondary line. Do not invent paragraphs, small English filler, pseudo-brands, archival codes, or watermarks.

## Failure Conditions

Regenerate or revise when any of the following appears:

- more visible objects or marks than the composition budget without a narrative reason;
- no clear focal subject or no readable path between groups;
- evenly scattered elements, contact-sheet layout, or rigid nine-grid presentation;
- full rectangular photographs pasted in without intentional paper treatment;
- dense fibers, dust, stains, mold, oil, water damage, burn marks, corrosion, heavy folds, or gray dirt overlays;
- worm-like, fingerprint, wave, wood-grain, hair-like, moire, or clustered microtexture;
- modern glossy vector, plastic, neon, 3D, floating-paper, or exaggerated-depth treatment;
- inconsistent shadows, noisy negative space, extra copy, malformed anatomy, or unstable architecture.

## Project Record

Use `style_preset: "clean-editorial"`. Record the selected accent colors and the frame-level composition budget in `project.json` so later frames preserve the same visual discipline.
